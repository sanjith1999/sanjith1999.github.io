DSP / FPGA Engineer

I build communication systems for a living, which means I spend a fair amount of time recovering information that physics has made difficult to find.

Somewhere along the way, I became fascinated by a bigger question: how much of an inverse problem is inherently difficult, and how much of that difficulty comes from the way we choose to model, measure, or recover information? My curiosity takes me from computational imaging to wireless sensing, and that question keeps me happily puzzled.
